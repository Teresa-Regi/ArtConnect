The Great Wave off Kanagawa

Katsushika Hokusai

Year: 1831

The Great Wave off Kanagawa is one of the world's most famous Japanese woodblock prints. It depicts towering waves threatening boats while Mount Fuji appears calmly in the distance. The artwork symbolizes the immense power of nature and the resilience of people.
