The Sleeping Gypsy

Henri Rousseau

Year: 1897

The Sleeping Gypsy shows a traveler peacefully sleeping in the desert while a lion quietly watches nearby. The dreamlike atmosphere and imaginative composition make it one of Rousseau's most iconic works.
