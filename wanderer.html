Wanderer Above the Sea of Fog

Caspar David Friedrich

Year: 1818

Wanderer Above the Sea of Fog is one of the greatest paintings of the Romantic era. It portrays a lone traveler standing on a rocky peak while looking over a mist-covered landscape. The artwork represents self-discovery, adventure, and the beauty of nature.
