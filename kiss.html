The Kiss

Gustav Klimt

Year: 1908

The Kiss is one of Gustav Klimt's most celebrated masterpieces. Rich golden patterns surround a couple sharing an embrace, symbolizing love, beauty, and intimacy. The artwork is famous for its decorative style and use of gold leaf.
